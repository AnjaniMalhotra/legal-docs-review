§ 07The pipeline

From PDF to signed-off review.

1–4 · Find. Cut the PDF into clauses, embed, pick the best 6 per standard.

5 · Read. The model labels one standard at a time.

6–8 · Decide. Python verifies and scores; a lawyer signs.
