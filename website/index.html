Why private payroll

Most payroll leaks don't start at your payroll provider.

They start at their vendors: the cloud host, the backup company, the file-transfer tool, the outsourced call centre. Every extra company in the chain is another place your employees' salaries, bank accounts and PAN numbers can escape.

PayNest was built to remove the chain entirely. Your data goes from your HR team to our servers and to your bank, and nowhere else.
