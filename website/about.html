About PayNest

We built payroll with no vendor's vendor.

PayNest Technologies is a Bengaluru company of about 450 people. We run payroll for 400+ organisations, and we do it all ourselves.

Our story

A leak that didn't start where anyone was looking.

In 2023, some of the world's best-known employers lost their staff's payroll data. Their own systems were never breached. The attackers got in through a file-transfer tool used by their payroll provider: a vendor's vendor that most of them had never heard of.

Our founders had spent years running payroll operations in India, and they saw the same chain everywhere: payroll software on someone else's cloud, backups at another company, support at a third, file transfers through a fourth.

PayNest's answer was simple: remove the chain. We own the servers, run the data centres, employ the support team and connect directly to the banks. When we tell a hospital or a bank where its employees' data lives, the answer fits in one sentence.

What we believe

Our values

Fewer hands

Every extra company that touches data is a risk. We keep the count at one.

Promises we can keep

We only sign commitments we can meet: 24-hour breach notice, not "immediately".

Payday is sacred

People plan their lives around salary day. We treat every run as if it were our own.

Show, don't tell

Audits, certificates, logs. Clients should be able to verify everything we claim.
