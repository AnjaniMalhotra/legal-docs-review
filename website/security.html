Our promise

Your payroll data never leaves PayNest.

Not to a cloud provider. Not to a backup company. Not to a call centre. Not to an AI tool. Here is exactly how we keep that promise, and what we commit to in writing.

Architecture

One company. Two data centres. No one else.

Every server that touches your data is owned and operated by PayNest.

  • DC-1 Bengaluru: primary payroll processing, employee app and support systems
  • DC-2 Pune: encrypted backups and disaster recovery, 840 km away on a separate power grid
  • Private link between the two sites; no public internet in between
  • Direct bank links for salary files; no file-transfer vendors

Controls

Security you can audit, not just trust.

ISO 27001 certified

Independent annual audit of our information security management system. The report is available to clients under NDA.

Encrypted everywhere

AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and hardware security modules for keys, held in our own data centres.

Least-privilege access

Role-based access, two-factor login, quarterly access reviews and named, background-verified staff only.

Complete audit logs

Every view, change and export is logged with user, time and reason, and logs are retained for one year.

No public AI tools

Staff may not paste client data into ChatGPT or any cloud AI. Internal AI tools run only on PayNest hardware.

24×7 monitoring

Our own security operations team watches both data centres around the clock. No outsourced SOC.

What we always do

  • Store and process your data only in India
  • Encrypt every backup before it leaves DC-1
  • Background-verify every employee with data access
  • Test disaster recovery twice a year
  • Give you a full export whenever you ask

What we never do

  • Host your data on a public cloud
  • Share your data with marketing or analytics companies
  • Outsource support to a third-party call centre
  • Use your data to train AI models
  • Track visitors to this website (no cookies, no analytics)
