Privacy policy

PayNest Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("PayNest", "we") processes two kinds of personal data: client employee data, which we process on behalf of our clients to run payroll, and business contact data, such as details you submit through this website.

Where data is processed

All personal data is stored and processed only on servers owned and operated by PayNest, in our data centres in Bengaluru (primary) and Pune (backup), India. We do not use public cloud providers or third-party data processors.

How we use data

  • Client employee data is used solely to deliver the payroll services our clients contract for.
  • Business contact data is used only to respond to your request and manage our relationship with you.
  • We never sell personal data, never share it for marketing, and never use it to train AI models.

Retention

Client data is returned and deleted within 30 days of contract termination, with backups purged in the next scheduled rotation, except where law requires longer retention of statutory records. Website enquiries are deleted after 24 months of inactivity.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data. Employees of our clients should contact their employer's HR team first, and we will assist them. Write to privacy@paynest.example.

Standard terms of service (summary)

These are the key positions in PayNest's standard client agreement. Where a client proposes its own agreement, our legal team reviews it against these positions.

  1. Liability. Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim. Indirect and consequential losses are excluded.
  2. Indemnity. Mutual, limited to third-party claims caused by a party's proven breach or negligence, and subject to the liability cap.
  3. Security incidents. We notify the client within 24 hours of confirming an incident affecting its data, followed by a written report within 72 hours.
  4. Service levels. 99.5% monthly availability, with service credits as described below.
  5. Audits. One on-site audit per year on 30 days' written notice, or our annual ISO 27001 audit report.
  6. Data return and deletion. Within 30 days of termination, with a deletion certificate on request.
  7. Confidentiality. Mutual, surviving three years after termination. We never share client data with third parties.
  8. Payment. Invoices payable within 30 days. Services may be suspended after 60 days' non-payment, with notice.
  9. Termination. Either party may terminate for convenience with 90 days' written notice.
  10. Governing law. Laws of India; courts at Bengaluru have exclusive jurisdiction.
